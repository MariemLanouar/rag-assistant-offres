Presentation of ScaleX Invest

ScaleX Invest is a SaaS platform for Venture Capital, Growth Equity and Private Equity investors to centralise portfolio data and make better decisions on valuation, risk and performance.

Building on Early Metrics’ decade of data and modelling expertise (since 2014), we combine a unique private-company dataset with validated methodologies to turn complex portfolio information into actionable insights for investment teams.

Our platform offers three core solutions:

Portfolio Monitoring: Real-time insights with risk and liquidity scoring for precise portfolio oversight and proactive risk management.
Multi-Asset Valuation: Accurate, flexible valuations powered by exclusive private deal data and bespoke modelling for equity, debt, convertible instruments, and more.
Venture Loan Scoring: Advanced scoring models, backed by a decade of testing, assess risk and ESG factors to guide smarter, compliant lending decisions.
